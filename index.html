01 — The person behind the pixels

Learning by making.

A little context

“The best way to understand an idea is to try building it.”

A reminder I keep close when a new concept feels bigger than it is.

I’m a third-year computer science and engineering student who loves the moment a blank page starts to become something useful.

Right now, I’m finding my feet in programming fundamentals and web development. I enjoy untangling a tricky problem, noticing the details that make a page feel welcoming, and learning from every version that doesn’t quite work yet.

Outside the editor, I’m usually reading, collecting ideas in my notebook, or looking for a new place to walk. I believe being a good developer starts with staying curious about people.
